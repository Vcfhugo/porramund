# Porra Mundial 2026

## Product Purpose
Fantasy football competition app for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Friends form private leagues, each picks 11 national teams across 6 custom groups, a Duo Dinamico (2 players whose goals+assists = bonus points), and top scorers from Group 1 "La Grasa Papa". Points accumulate automatically as the tournament progresses. A pot (10€/person) goes to the highest scorer.

## Users
Close friend groups and office pools. Spanish-speaking. Age 25–45. Casual football fans who care more about the social drama and competition than deep stats. Use on mobile primarily, check results at work on desktop. The deadline (June 11) creates real urgency.

## Register
product

## Brand Tone
Bold, energetic, a little cocky. This is a bet between friends, not a corporate product. Spanish slang is fine. Humor is appropriate. Should feel like a WhatsApp group that got a UI.

## Color Strategy
Full palette — Brazil 2014 official colors translated to modern flat design. Gold (#FDD301 / oklch equivalent) as primary action color, deep navy (#001428) as background, Rose Madder (#D8131A) for danger/live, American Orange (#F79516) for warnings/secondary actions, Juicy Lime (#ABCB2D) for positive/nature signals. No generic "safe" palette. This should feel unmistakably World Cup.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with teal + white
- Fantasy sports apps that look like spreadsheets
- News sites with sidebar ads
- Bootstrap-default anything
- Glassmorphism for decoration

## Strategic Principles
1. The deadline (11 June 17:00) is the most urgent UX element — it should be impossible to miss.
2. "Mis equipos" is the identity of the user's experience — show it proudly everywhere.
3. The social layer (who did what, rankings) is as important as the competition itself.
4. The Reglamento must be 1 tap away at all times — confusion about rules is a product failure.
5. Web view is for desktop check-ins: standings, bracket, activity. Mobile is for making picks.
